Chemistry · iPhone and Android

Chemistry clicks when you build it yourself.

Spark Chemistry turns the periodic table, ions, formulas and equations into short games. Hunt elements, build compounds, balance reactions: a few minutes a day, reviews at the right moment, and progress you can actually see.

Coming soon on the App Store and Google Play.

What's inside

Four worlds to explore

Atoms and elements, ions, compounds and their names, then reactions. Each world is a path of short lessons; finish one and its world challenge waits for you.

Three games, one idea: you do the chemistry

Element Hunter: find the element on the table. Compound Builder: put ions together until the charges add up. Reaction Balancer: set the coefficients until every atom is counted.

Tools that stay with you

A periodic table, a molar-mass calculator and an equation balancer, ready for homework, even offline and without an account.

Free to learn

Every world is free. The free version shows a few ads between sessions, never during an exercise. Spark Premium removes the ads.
